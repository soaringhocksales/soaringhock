About The Business

Soaring Hock Sales

Soaring Hock Sales is a small resale business focused on buying, consigning, and reselling useful, collectible, vintage, and interesting items.

What we do

We help give good items another chance. If you are cleaning out a garage, downsizing, moving, clearing storage, or getting ready to donate or throw things away, contact us first. We may be interested in buying items directly or helping sell them through consignment.

We look for items that still have life, usefulness, resale value, or collector interest. That can include electronics, tools, video games, media, collectibles, vintage items, household goods, outdoor gear, hobby items, and other pieces that someone else may still want.

Why contact us first?

A lot of valuable or useful items get donated, tossed, or left sitting in storage simply because people do not know what to do with them. Before you haul things away or drop them off at a thrift store, send us a few photos and a short description. We can let you know if it is something we are interested in.

Our goal is to keep the process simple: show us what you have, tell us where you are located, and we will go from there.

How we sell

Soaring Hock Sales resells items through online marketplaces, including eBay, and may also feature select items through live sales, local contacts, or other resale channels. This website is the central place to learn what we do, see examples of current inventory, and contact us about items you want to sell.
